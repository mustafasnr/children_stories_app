Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application "Children Stories" (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"). The Application is designed to provide engaging and educational stories for children. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Application, we may collect certain information, which may include:

  • Account Information: If you register or log in, we may collect your email address and profile details through our backend provider.
  • Purchase Information: To manage premium subscriptions and in-app purchases, transaction details (excluding payment card numbers) and subscription status are processed.
  • Usage and Device Data: We may collect non-personal information such as device model, operating system version, and general usage statistics to improve app performance and user experience.

2. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to support the functionality of the Application. These service providers have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose:

  • Supabase: Used for database management, authentication, and backend services. You can view their privacy policy on the Supabase website.
  • Adapty: Used to manage in-app purchases, subscriptions, and paywalls. You can view their privacy policy on the Adapty website.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The Application is designed for children. However, registration, account creation, and in-app purchases are intended to be performed and managed solely by parents or legal guardians.

We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the EU) without verifiable parental consent. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under the relevant age without parental consent, we will delete that information as quickly as possible. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, please be aware that no security measures are completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us.
